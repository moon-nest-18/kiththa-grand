Our Heritage

Kiththa Grand was born from a deep love for Sri Lanka's legendary spice heritage. For centuries, Ceylon was known as the "Island of Spices" — and we're here to bring that legacy to kitchens around the world.

Every spice is hand-selected from trusted farmers, sun-dried traditionally, and packed with care — preserving the aroma, flavour, and potency that makes Ceylon spices world-famous.
